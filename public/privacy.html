Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Magyar Learning is an educational website for practising everyday and medical Hungarian. This policy describes the current guest experience and the optional account and synchronization features being prepared for a future update. Account-related processing described below applies when those features are enabled.

1. Information we use

Guest learning progress, including vocabulary mastery, quiz statistics, review items and the last studied module, is saved in your browser's local storage. Guest progress is not currently synchronized to a server. If you export progress, the resulting backup file is yours to store or transfer.

When account features are enabled, email registration will use your email address and a password handled by our authentication provider, Supabase. Magyar Learning does not need to store a separate copy of your password.

If you choose Google sign-in, we will request basic identity information (OpenID, email and profile). Google may provide your account identifier, email address, name and profile picture. We use this information to identify and authenticate your account, display your account information where relevant, and associate your learning progress with the correct account. We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, contacts or other Google services.

2. How information is used

Account information and learning records are used to provide sign-in, password recovery, learning continuity, and progress synchronization between devices. Learning records may include study statistics, mistakes, completed modules, preferred voice and study settings. The site is for educational practice, not patient records; please do not submit patient or other sensitive personal information.

3. Storage and service providers

Cloudflare hosts the website and pronunciation files. Supabase provides authentication and database services for the planned account features, with the project database located in Frankfurt, Germany. Google provides optional identity authentication. These providers may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate and secure their services. A transactional email provider may be used to deliver account verification and password recovery messages when email login is made available.

We do not sell Google account information, use it for advertising, or share it with unrelated third parties for their own marketing. Google user data is used only to provide and secure the requested account functionality.

4. Security and browser storage

The hosted website uses HTTPS. The planned cloud progress database uses access policies that restrict students to their own records. Browser storage will retain guest progress and, when accounts are enabled, may retain authentication sessions and account-specific cached progress. Avoid keeping a signed-in session on a shared device.

5. Retention and your choices

Guest progress remains in your browser until you clear the website's stored data or reset it through available website controls. You may export your progress before doing so. When cloud synchronization is enabled, account information and learning progress are retained to provide the account service until deletion is requested, subject to necessary security, legal and provider backup retention. You may request access, correction or deletion of your account data from the app operator. You can also remove Magyar Learning's Google access from your Google Account connections; this does not by itself delete any learning records previously saved to your account.

6. Contact and updates

For privacy questions or account-data requests, contact the Magyar Learning operator using the user support email displayed on the application's Google sign-in consent screen, or contact the operator who shared the website with you. Please do not include passwords or authentication codes in your message. We will update this policy as account features and service providers change.
