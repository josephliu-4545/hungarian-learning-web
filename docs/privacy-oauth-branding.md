# Privacy and Google OAuth branding

The static policy is public/privacy.html, copied to both Vite build outputs. The app footer links to it. The policy distinguishes current guest storage from planned account processing; it must be updated and reviewed when account features are activated.

Before publishing or enabling student authentication, the operator must confirm their controller identity, public privacy/support contact email, email provider, retention/deletion procedure and the final privacy disclosures. Do not publish a student's or developer's private email without permission. This initial policy refers to the configured Google consent-screen support email; replace or supplement this with a confirmed direct contact. No claim of legal compliance or Google verification is made.

After rebuilding and uploading dist-beta, verify the homepage and /privacy.html open without login. Enter these exact public URLs in Google Auth Platform Branding, with the selected support email and developer contact. Google may additionally require authorized-domain or brand verification; do not claim ownership of workers.dev or supabase.co parent domains. If necessary, verify the controlled subdomain where Google supports it or use a domain owned by the operator.
